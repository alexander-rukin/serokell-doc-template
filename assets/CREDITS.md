# Asset provenance

Every non-code file in `assets/` and where it comes from. Add a line here when
you add an asset - an unattributed image in a public repository is a takedown
waiting to happen.

## Brand artwork and marks

`serokell-mark-*.{png,svg}`, `brand-mountains.png`, `brand-peak.png`,
`brand-clouds.png`, `brand-footer-long.png`

Serokell OÜ property. They are **not** covered by the repository's Apache-2.0
licence - see `NOTICE`. They are here so the template can render Serokell
documents, not for reuse elsewhere.

| Google Sans Flex | SIL OFL 1.1 (`OFL-GoogleSansFlex.txt`) | Google Fonts |
| Golos Text | SIL OFL 1.1 (`OFL-GolosText.txt`) | https://github.com/googlefonts/golos-text |
| JetBrains Mono | SIL OFL 1.1 (`OFL-JetBrainsMono.txt`) | https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono |
| Noto Color Emoji | SIL OFL 1.1 (`OFL-NotoColorEmoji.txt`) | Google Fonts |

The OFL permits bundling and redistribution as long as the licence travels with
the font, which is what those `.txt` files are for. Do not drop them.

## Photography

`sample-photo.jpg` - "Fog over the Alaska Range, frost on brush and spruce
trees". Photograph by Tim Rains, Denali National Park and Preserve (US National
Park Service). **Public domain** (work of the US federal government).
Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Alaska_Range-_Fog_light.jpg
Resized to 1400px wide and re-encoded; not otherwise altered.

It is the only photograph in the repository, and it exists so the image layouts
have something real to show in `decks/all-layouts.md`. A photograph with no
recorded licence must not replace it.
